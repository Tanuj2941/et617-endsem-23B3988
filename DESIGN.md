# Pendulum Explorer – Educational App Design

## Exam Card
- Problem: P5 – Inquiry-based Virtual Laboratory
- Topic: T1 – Simple Pendulum
- Learner: L5 – Anxious repeat learner
- Constraint: C3 – Mobile-first, one-handed
- Identity: 23B3988

## Educational Problem
Class 9 students often struggle to understand how pendulum length, mass and amplitude affect its time period. Students who previously failed this topic may hesitate to experiment because they fear making mistakes. This virtual laboratory provides a safe environment where learners can test predictions, manipulate variables and learn from incorrect experiments.

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of the activity, learners will be able to:

1. Predict how pendulum length affects its time period.
2. Compare the effects of mass and amplitude on the pendulum's period.
3. Conduct experiments, interpret results and revise incorrect hypotheses.

## Learner Persona
- Name: Aarav
- Age: 15
- Class: 9
- Background: Previously failed the pendulum topic and feels anxious about physics.
- Prior knowledge: Basic measurement of time, length and mass.
- Misconception 1: A heavier pendulum swings faster.
- Misconception 2: Increasing pendulum length decreases its time period.
- Device: Android smartphone
- Language: English
- Motivation: Improve understanding and pass the next physics test.

## Learning Activity
1. Introduction to simple pendulums.
2. Predict how changing length affects the period.
3. Adjust length, mass and amplitude using sliders.
4. Run a simulated experiment and observe results.
5. Compare predictions with actual results.
6. Receive supportive feedback and hints.
7. Revise hypotheses and retry.
8. Complete a short assessment.

## Design Constraint: C3
The application will:
- Work on screens as narrow as 360px.
- Use large touch-friendly buttons and sliders.
- Place primary controls within comfortable thumb reach.
- Avoid hover-only interactions.

## AI Functionality
AI will provide contextual explanations and feedback based on learner predictions and experimental results. A predefined feedback fallback will be available if the AI service fails.

## Evaluation
Learners will complete five assessment tasks using prediction, multiple-choice and short-answer formats. Success means correctly explaining the effects of length, mass and small amplitude on pendulum period and improving after misconception-based feedback.
